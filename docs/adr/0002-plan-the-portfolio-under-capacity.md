# 2. Plan the portfolio under reviewer capacity, not each use case on its own

**Context.** Public-sector pilots stall less on technology than on reviews: one data protection
officer, one security officer, a staff council that decides once a month, a procurement office. A
timeline per use case hides that four use cases queue for the same reviewer.

**Decision.** Gates are scheduled week by week in priority order with each customer's reviewer
capacity, staff council sessions, Christmas blackouts, and OpenAI Deployment Engineering capacity
shared across the whole portfolio. Levers (merge the FRIA into the DPIA under Art. 27(4), one more
reviewer, a framework call-off) are priced by replanning, not estimated.

**Consequences.** The plan names the bottleneck and what fixing it buys. Durations are planning
defaults the customer confirms in discovery; the plan is as good as those inputs.
